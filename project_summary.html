Indian Railways Dynamic ETA & Operations Suite

Next-Gen Machine Learning Fleet Radar, Voice AI & 24/7 Cloud Dispatcher
Project Technical Summary • Southern Railway (MAS Division)

1. The Core Problem (Indian Railways-la Real-ah Enna Problem?)

  • Static Average Calculations: Ippo irukura Where Is My Train matrum NTES ellam pazhaiya station departure timestamp vechu linear average speed-la dhaan calculate pannudhu.
  • Lack of Sectional Dynamics: Rendu station-ku naduvula (block section-la) pogum pothu:
    • Signal aspect enna color-la irukku (Red / Double Yellow / Green).
    • Munnadi pora train-oda distance (Headway separation & spatial clearance).
    • Temporary Speed Restrictions (TSR).
    • Rain & rail adhesion (vazhukkum track).
    • Locomotive traction link (WAP-7 fast acceleration vs Heavy Freight WAG-9).
    • Precedence loops & freight siding holds.
    Indha real ground parameters-ah edhumey track panni ETA calculate panradhu illa.
  • Cascade Ripple Delay: Oru main junction-la (eg. Arakkonam or Basin Bridge) oru superfast train 15 mins delay aana, adhuku pinnaadi vara moththa train-kum vara downstream cascade delay-ah predict panna mudiyala.
  • Terminal Bottlenecks: MAS Chennai Central maadhiri big terminals-la platform clash aaguradhu last minute-la dhaan theriyudhu, adhanala outer-la trains hold aagudhu.

2. Solution Expected (Judges & Ministry Enna Expect Pannanga?)

  • Dynamic ML Prediction: Linear calculation illama, real-time section variables vechu accurate dynamic ETA predict panna vendiyadhu.
  • Track-Geometry GIS Snapped Radar: Map-la straight lines illama exact railway curvature track mela train move aaga vendiyadhu.
  • Ripple Delay Forecasting: Oru train late aana adhoda ripple effect pinnaadi vara express trains-ah epdi affect pannum nu network-wide predict pannanum.
  • Platform Conflict Resolver: Chennai Central-la platform clash aagaama smart auto-reallocation nadakkanum.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Delay-ku enna reason nu clear-ah sollanum (Signal Aspect, Rail Adhesion drop, TSR restriction).
  • Passenger Accessibility: Multilingual Voice AI and internet illadhavangalukku direct offline SMS/Email alert dispatch system.

3. What We Have Built (Namba Build Panni Cloud-la Potrukura Complete System)

A. Machine Learning & Predictive Analytics Engine

  • XGBoost Regressor Model: Real multi-factor dataset vechu train panna ML model with R² = 99.70% accuracy and 1.79 mins MAE.
  • Cascade Ripple Propagation Algorithm: Primary delay-ah calculate panni secondary downstream trains-oda buffer impact-ah direct-ah forecast pannudhu.
  • Explainable AI (XAI) Parser: Mathematical prediction-ah human language reason-ah decode pannudhu (eg. "7 min hold due to Yellow Signal at Jolarpettai").

B. Real-Time Sectional Telemetry & GIS Radar

  • Curvature-Snapped Multi-Corridor Tracking: MAS-CBE, MAS-SBC, MAS-NDLS, MAS-HWH, and MAS-VLNK tracks mela 14 express & freight rakes 1-second continuous telemetry-oda run aagudhu.
  • True Rolling Stock Intelligence: Engine link details (RPM WAP-7, AJJ WAP-4, Erode WAG-9HC, Vande Bharat Train-18), Rake Sharing (RSA), and 06000 series Special trains integrate aagirukku.
  • MAS Platforms 1-12 Live Interlocking Board: Chennai Central-oda 12 platforms-layum arrival/departure status and automated conflict resolver ready-ah irukku.

C. Passenger Multi-Channel System & Cloud Architecture

  • Multilingual Voice AI Copilot: Tamil, English, Hindi, Telugu, and Kannada-la voice mic moolama pesi audio voice response ketka mudiyum.
  • Automated SMTP Email & Twilio SMS Gateway: User email & phone number kudutha platform alert & ETA instantaneous-ah direct-ah deliver aagidum.
  • 24/7 Cloud Host & PWA: Render server-la backend ML engine 24/7 non-stop run aagudhu, laptop off aanaalum live-ah irukum, mobile-layum direct app-ah install pannikalam.
