XGBoost Loss & Objective Function

The model minimizes second-order Taylor expansion loss to evaluate residual sectional delays across decision trees:

$$\mathcal{L}^{(t)} \approx \sum_{i=1}^n \left[ g_i f_t(x_i) + \frac{1}{2} h_i f_t^2(x_i) \right] + \gamma T + \frac{1}{2} \lambda \sum_{j=1}^T w_j^2$$

Where $g_i$ and $h_i$ are first and second order gradients of sectional travel time errors, with regularization penalties.

Dynamic ETA Physics Calculation Formula

Actual arrival time is synthesized by aggregating physical track distance against dynamic rolling stock constraints:

$$\text{ETA}_{\text{mins}} = \left(\frac{\text{Distance}_{\text{rem}}}{\text{Speed}_{\text{live}}}\right) \times 60 + P_{\text{signal}} + P_{\text{headway}} + P_{\text{adhesion}}$$

Where penalties are scaled dynamically based on live signal aspects, weather adhesion loss, and locomotive acceleration capability factors.
